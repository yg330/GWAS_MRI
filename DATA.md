# Data

Included in `Data/` (each under 25 MB): significant-SNP tables (`UKB_sig_info.txt`, `Meta_sig_info.txt`), heritability
table, genetic correlation matrix, FLAMES results (from Supplementary Table 4), the supplementary tables workbook,
eRegulon and cell-type tables for the developing cortex (Wang et al.), reference maps (Sydnor et al.; Hansen et al. AHBA),
published arealisation gene sets (Qian et al.; Bhaduri et al.), clinical gene panels, emVar and ABC tables, and
gene coordinates (NCBI37.3).

`Outputs/` holds intermediate results that later scripts read, plus cached objects in `Outputs/cache/`.
Figures are not stored; re-run the scripts.
